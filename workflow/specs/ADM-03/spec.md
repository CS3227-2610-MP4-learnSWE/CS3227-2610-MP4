# Spec: ADM-03 AI feedback analysis

Status: Approved by user in chat, 2026-11-09 (recommended defaults accepted). Amendment 2026-11-09 (user): stored analyses are kept indefinitely and admins can delete feedback responses (section "Amendment 2026-11-09 (user)" below); status stays approved, the amendment's open questions A1 to A5 are recommended defaults for the human to confirm.

## Goal
US-ADM-03-01: an admin selects feedback responses they may review (any number, up to a form's whole audience of 500) and gets, at once, statistics and diagrams computed by the backend, followed by an AI summary, recurring issues by theme and suggested improvements with evidence references. US-ADM-03-02: the admin opens exactly the response cited by a finding to check the evidence. Analysis is advisory only.

## Behaviour
Screen "Feedback analysis" at `/admin/feedback/analysis` (admin only), linked from the admin Feedback area.

1. The admin chooses one feedback form and sees its responses as a paged checklist (submission time and respondent name are visible to the admin, as in ADM-02-04). They tick any number of responses, or use "Select all", then press "Generate analysis".
2. Select all: one checkbox "Select all {n} responses" selects every response of the form across all pages. n comes from an ADM-03-owned endpoint (`GET /api/admin/feedback/forms/{form_id}/response-count`, plan.md endpoint 5) because the ADM-02 list has no total; it is at most 500. The checkbox is disabled, with text "Counting responses...", until the count has loaded. While it is on, individual checkboxes are shown checked and disabled, the label reads "All {n} responses selected", and a button "Clear selection" turns it off and clears everything. A button "Refresh list" reloads the list and the count; if new responses arrived the count updates and Select all stays on.
3. The create request carries either an explicit `response_ids` list or `select_all: true` (never both). For select all, the server resolves the ids at creation to a fixed snapshot ordered by submission time then id, and stores it. If responses were added or removed between page load and submit, the analysis covers the snapshot taken at submit and reports the exact number analysed; if that differs from the count the admin saw, the result shows "You selected {expected} responses; {actual} were analysed because responses changed after you opened the list." The browser sends the count it displayed as `expected_count` (select all only).
4. Statistics are instant and independent of the AI. In the same request that creates the analysis, the backend computes the statistics with plain functions (no LLM, no batches, no network): per question the answered and skipped counts, option counts and percentages for single-choice (`multiple_choice`, `dropdown`) and multi-choice (`checkbox`) questions, and count, mean, median, minimum, maximum and distribution for `linear_scale` questions; free-text questions (`short_answer`, `paragraph`) get answered and skipped counts. Because a form can change between responses, each response is read with the question definitions in its own stored form snapshot and questions are grouped by question id (rule in plan.md "Data model"). The result is stored and returned by the create request (HTTP 201) with `aggregates` filled. Expected time for 500 responses: under 1 second on the server; the tested upper bound is 3 seconds; the work is one read of at most 500 response rows plus one pass over their answers, with no outbound call.
5. The page `/admin/feedback/analysis/:analysisId` therefore shows the charts immediately, under the heading "Question results" with the note "Computed from the selected responses without AI." The "AI analysis" section below it then fills in. The states are:
   - Statistics ready and AI running: charts shown; the AI section shows "AI analysis running. Step {done} of {total}: {step}."
   - Statistics ready and AI failed: charts stay visible and are still labelled as computed without AI; the failure reason appears only in the AI section; no summary, themes or suggestions are shown.
   - Statistics ready and AI complete: charts plus Summary, Recurring issues (each "Reported in feedback" with evidence chips) and Suggested improvements (each "Based on feedback" with chips, or "AI suggestion (no supporting feedback)").
   A partial AI result is never shown as complete.
6. What the AI does and why there are map calls: the model is used only for what functions cannot do, the qualitative themes, summary and suggestions from the free-text answers. The shared LLM client accepts at most 12,000 characters per call, so free-text answers go to the model in batches. These batches are the "map" calls (at most 10 per analysis): each takes one batch of free-text answers and returns a few partial themes. One final "reduce" call merges the partial themes into the summary, themes and suggestions. Nothing numeric is computed by the model; choice and scale answers are never sent to the model. The only numbers the reduce call receives are the already computed statistics as a compact context line per question, to word the summary; the model never calculates or changes them (see Open questions). The shared client has no tool calling, so there is no tool use.
7. Map-reduce bounds. Free-text answers are normalised (trimmed, whitespace collapsed, delimiter strings removed) and de-duplicated per question (identical text from several responses becomes one entry citing up to 5 labels, the first 5 by number). Each entry is cut to 280 characters of answer text. Entries are packed into batches of at most 9,000 data characters and 30 entries; batch instruction text is at most 1,500 characters, so each map call is at most 10,500 characters. At most 10 map calls run, so at most 300 entries. If there are more unique entries than fit, the server takes a deterministic evenly spaced sample (entries ordered by first label number then question position; sample size reduced in 10 percent steps until at most 10 batches are needed) and the result states "AI themes based on {N} of {M} free-text answers." whenever N is below M; never silently. The reduce call receives only the statistics context (at most 1,500 characters) and the partial themes (at most 6,150 characters), never raw answers. With no free-text entries there are no map calls and one reduce call. Labels R1..Rn are global for the analysis (n at most 500) so evidence from any batch is validated against the stored snapshot.
8. AI job. After the 201 response the server runs the AI calls one after another in an in-process background task. Progress is calls finished out of (map calls + 1) and is read by polling. Worst case is 10 map calls of two attempts at 30 seconds each plus the retry pause, 10 x (2 x 30 + 1) = 610 seconds, and one reduce call of two attempts at 60 seconds, 2 x 60 + 1 = 121 seconds: 731 seconds, so the AI step has a hard bound of 780 seconds. Any call that fails after its allowed attempts fails the whole AI step with that call's specific error (one job-level retry for model-output failures only, see Failure messages; no partial result). A limit hit in a batch fails it with the limit's name and wait. An AI step still `running` after 780 seconds (for example after a server restart) is turned into failed `analysis_interrupted` at the next read or create. One AI step may run per admin; cancel is out of scope. Refresh or Back on the result URL reloads state from the database and resumes polling.
9. Evidence chips (for example "R3") link to `/admin/feedback/analysis/:analysisId/responses/R3`, which shows that one response read-only. Only labels of the analysis can be opened.
10. History is permanent until the admin deletes an analysis. Every analysis (AI running, complete or failed) is stored in the database for the admin who created it, with its statistics, AI result or failure record, selection snapshot and label-to-response mapping. Nothing is pruned or expired automatically. "Previous analyses" lists them newest first, 20 per page, with "Load more analyses".
10a. Delete. An admin can permanently delete their own analysis with `DELETE /api/admin/feedback/analyses/{analysis_id}`, from a "Delete analysis" button on the result page or a delete button on each history row, after confirming in a dialog. It is a hard delete of the stored analysis row: statistics, AI result or failure record, selection snapshot and label-to-response mapping. NOT deleted: the underlying feedback responses and forms (unchanged), and ADM-06 AI usage counters (they stay). Another admin's analysis, an unknown id and an already-deleted id all give the same 404 `analysis_not_found`. An analysis whose AI step is still running cannot be deleted (409 `analysis_running`, with how long it has run and when it will be marked interrupted); it can be deleted once it is complete or failed (a running step older than 780 seconds is first marked interrupted, then deletable). After deletion the result page, the evidence pages and the list no longer show it: result and evidence URLs give the uniform not-found states. A late write from the background job to a deleted row changes nothing and raises no error.
11. Privacy: the model sees only opaque labels, question wording (cut to 60 characters) and free-text answer text. Never sent: user id, display name, response id, form id, form title, timestamps, choice or scale answers. Free text typed by respondents may contain names; not redacted (see Open questions).
12. Retry after a failure is a new analysis (new `request_id`; with Select all a new snapshot); statistics are recomputed. Same `request_id` from the same admin returns the existing analysis (HTTP 200, whatever its state, no second job). After a network failure on Generate the page keeps the same `request_id` for the next press, so a request that did reach the server is not duplicated.
13. Edge cases: a cited response later deleted: the analysis stays; opening that chip shows the `evidence_not_found` message with no content. A disabled admin is rejected by the existing admin check on every endpoint; a running AI step of theirs still finishes and is stored. Another admin cannot see or open this admin's analysis.

## Failure messages
No catch-all wording exists anywhere in this feature ("Something went wrong", "An error occurred", or "Try again later" with no reason). Every message states what failed, at which step, the concrete cause with numbers where known, and what the admin can do. Messages contain no response content and no identity. `{step}` is "batch {k} of {n}" for a map call, "merging themes" for the reduce call, "reading the responses" / "computing the statistics" before the AI starts, or "deleting the analysis" for a delete. Limits in messages (20 per minute, 300 per day, 15 seconds, 12,000 characters) are read from the shared client's limits, not hard-coded. The stored failure record keeps `code`, `message`, `step` and `details` so history shows the same reason later (plan.md "Failure record").

Request failures (returned by the create/read endpoints; the UI shows the message exactly):
- `invalid_request`: "The analysis request is malformed: {reason}." where reason is one of "form_id is not a valid id", "request_id is not a valid id", "response_ids must be a list of ids", "response_ids contains a value that is not a valid id", "send either response_ids or select_all, not both", "send response_ids or select_all set to true", "select_all must be true", "expected_count must be a whole number from 0 to 500", "expected_count is only allowed with select_all", "unknown field {name}", "the request body is larger than 256 KiB".
- `selection_required`: "No responses are selected. Select at least one response, or use Select all, then generate the analysis."
- `no_responses`: "This form has no submitted responses, so there is nothing to analyse."
- `too_many_responses`: "You selected {n} responses, but one analysis can cover at most 500. Select fewer responses."
- `nothing_to_analyse`: "The {n} selected responses contain no answered questions, so there is nothing to analyse."
- `form_not_found`: "That form is not available. It may have been deleted. Choose another form."
- `response_not_found`: "{k} of the {n} selected responses are not available for this form (they may have been deleted or belong to another form). Reload the list and select again."
- `analysis_in_progress` (409): "Another analysis started {s} seconds ago is still running its AI step. A running AI step finishes or fails within 780 seconds. Wait for it, then generate again."
- `analysis_not_found`: "That analysis is not available. It does not exist or is not in your history."
- `analysis_running` (409, delete only): "This analysis cannot be deleted yet: its AI step has been running for {s} seconds. It finishes or is marked interrupted within {r} seconds (the limit is 780 seconds). Delete it after that."
- Delete-specific page messages (client side): 404 on delete: "This analysis was already deleted or is not in your history. The list has been refreshed."; network failure: "Could not reach the server while deleting the analysis. It was not confirmed as deleted. Press Delete permanently to try again."; 15 s abort: "The server did not answer within 15 seconds while deleting the analysis. It may or may not be deleted. Press Delete permanently again; if it was already deleted you will be told so."; success status: "Analysis deleted. The original feedback responses and forms were not changed."
- `evidence_not_found`: "This response cannot be shown. It was deleted, or the reference is not part of this analysis."
- `internal_error` (500, create or read): "An unexpected server error occurred while {step}. The cause was not an invalid request. Reference {ref} identifies it in the server logs."
- Session and role: 401 "Your session has expired. Sign in again."; 403 "You do not have access to this page. Only admin accounts can use feedback analysis."

AI step failures (stored in the analysis; shown only in the AI section):
- `ai_timeout`: "The AI service did not answer within {t} seconds (after up to 2 attempts) while processing {step}. The statistics are unaffected. Start a new analysis to run the AI part again."
- `rate_limited_minute`: "The AI limit of {limit} requests per minute was reached while processing {step}. Try again in {n} seconds."
- `rate_limited_day`: "The daily AI limit of {limit} requests was reached while processing {step}. Try again in {n} seconds."
- `ai_busy`: "The AI service replied that it is busy (HTTP 429) while processing {step}. Try again in {n} seconds."
- `ai_unavailable`: "The AI service could not be reached or returned a server error while processing {step}, after up to 2 attempts. The statistics are unaffected. Start a new analysis once the service is back."
- `ai_not_configured`: "AI analysis is not configured on this server (no SoC LLM settings are present), so the AI part could not start. Ask the site operator to configure it. The statistics are unaffected."
- `ai_input_too_large`: "The AI input for {step} was {x} characters, over the limit of {limit}. This is a defect in the batch size, not something you can fix by selecting differently. Reference {ref} identifies it in the server logs."
- `ai_reply_not_json`: "The AI reply for {step} was not a single JSON object, so it was rejected. No AI result was saved. Start a new analysis."
- `ai_reply_invalid`: "The AI reply for {step} was rejected because {rule}. No AI result was saved. Start a new analysis." where rule is one of "an unexpected field ({field}) was present", "a required field ({field}) was missing", "{field} had the wrong type", "{field} was empty", "{field} was longer than {max} characters", "{field} had {count} items, the maximum is {max}", "a theme had no supporting responses", "a supporting response label ({label}) is not in the selection", "a supporting response label ({label}) was not in the input for this step", "a suggestion marked as based on feedback had no supporting responses", "a suggestion marked as an AI suggestion listed supporting responses", "a suggestion basis was not feedback or ai".
- `ai_reply_cut_off`: "The AI reply for {step} was cut off at the output limit of {max_tokens} tokens{note}. No AI result was saved. Select fewer responses and start a new analysis." where {note} is empty, or " (the model used its whole token budget before answering)" when the reply had no content at all and finish_reason was length.
- After two failed attempts (model-output failures `ai_reply_cut_off`, `ai_reply_not_json` and `ai_reply_invalid` only; the job retries each map call and the reduce call once, never for limits, busy, timeout, unavailable, not configured, input too large, responses deleted or internal errors) the stored failure is the second attempt's, its message gets the suffix " The step failed after 2 attempts." and its details gain `attempts` = 2 and `first_attempt_code`. Single-attempt messages are unchanged.
- `ai_time_limit`: "The AI step stopped before {step} because the analysis had already run {s} seconds, close to the {limit} second limit. The statistics remain correct. Start a new analysis, or select fewer responses." Details `elapsed_seconds`, `limit_seconds` (600), `step`; not retried.
- `analysis_interrupted`: "The AI step was interrupted: it had been running for {s} seconds without finishing (the limit is 780 seconds), probably because the server restarted. It had finished {done} of {total} steps. The statistics are unaffected. Start a new analysis."
- `internal_error` (stored): same text as above with the step that failed.

Page polling and loading failures (client side, exact):
- Network failure while checking progress: "Could not reach the server while checking the analysis progress. The analysis is saved and may still be running. Press Retry to check again."
- Unreadable status: "The server answered the progress check with an unreadable reply (HTTP {status}). The analysis is saved. Press Retry to check again."
- Stopped waiting: "Stopped waiting after 810 seconds. The server may still be working. Reload this page to check again."
- Network failure on any other load: "Could not reach the server while loading {what}. Check your connection and press Retry." with {what} one of "forms", "responses", "the response count", "previous analyses", "the analysis", "the response".
- Browser abort after 15 seconds: "The server did not answer within 15 seconds while loading {what}. Press Retry."
- Network failure or abort on Generate: "Could not reach the server while starting the analysis. If it did start it will appear in Previous analyses. Press Generate analysis again; repeating it will not start a second copy." (abort: "The server did not answer within 15 seconds while starting the analysis." followed by the same last two sentences).

## Acceptance criteria
Copied from docs/UserStories.md.

US-ADM-03-01
- [ ] AC1 Input selection: I can select responses for analysis, and the analysis uses only selected responses that I am authorised to review.
- [ ] AC2 Result sections: The result contains a summary, recurring issues grouped by theme, and suggested improvements.
- [ ] AC3 Fact versus suggestion: The result distinguishes issues reported in the selected feedback from AI-generated suggestions.
- [ ] AC4 Evidence references: Each grouped issue includes references to supporting responses in the selected input. A suggestion presented as based on specific feedback also references its supporting responses; a suggestion without such evidence is identified as an AI suggestion, not a reported finding.
- [ ] AC5 Empty input: If no responses are selected, the system does not generate an analysis and tells me that a selection is required.
- [ ] AC6 Failure states: If a request limit is reached or analysis fails, the system tells me which occurred and does not present a partial or failed result as complete.
- [ ] AC7 Scope boundary: Analysis is advisory only; it does not edit forms or responses, send messages, or apply suggested changes.

US-ADM-03-02
- [ ] AC8 Reference target: Selecting a supporting-response reference opens the exact response cited by that finding.
- [ ] AC9 Access restriction: The response is displayed only if I remain authorised to access it; an invalid, deleted, or inaccessible reference does not reveal response content.
- [ ] AC10 Scope boundary: This story opens only responses cited by an analysis. Browsing all responses for a form is covered by ADM-02-04.
- [ ] AC11 No side effects: Opening a finding or response does not send a message or modify the analysis, form, or response.

Added by this spec (derived from SHR-01, SHR-02, SHR-04, SHR-05 and the human feedback)
- [ ] AC12 Every ADM-03 endpoint rejects unauthenticated requests (401) and non-admin or disabled users, with no body data, before any lookup, job or LLM call.
- [ ] AC13 An analysis and its evidence are readable only by the admin who created it; another admin gets `analysis_not_found` or `evidence_not_found` (404).
- [ ] AC14 No prompt (map or reduce) contains a user id, display name, response id, form id, form title or timestamp.
- [ ] AC15 Every LLM reply (map and reduce) is strict JSON validated against its schema; a violation fails the AI step with `ai_reply_not_json` or `ai_reply_invalid` naming the rule, and nothing is presented as a complete AI result.
- [ ] AC16 Text inside responses that tries to change the output format, reveal the prompt or request actions cannot change the validated schema or cause any write other than storing the analysis.
- [ ] AC17 There is no 20-response cap: an explicit list of up to 500 ids, or `select_all`, is accepted; more than 500 ids, an empty list, ids of another form or unknown ids are rejected with their exact messages and no job and no LLM call; duplicate ids are deduplicated.
- [ ] AC18 Select all resolves to a fixed snapshot of every response of the form at creation (across pages); the analysis reports the exact number analysed; `response_ids` and `select_all` together are rejected.
- [ ] AC19 Same `request_id` twice gives one analysis and one AI job; two simultaneous submits with different `request_id` from one admin give one 201 and one 409.
- [ ] AC20 The create response already contains correct aggregates (counts, percentages, mean, median, answered, skipped) computed by backend functions without the LLM; for 500 responses the create request completes within 3 seconds with the LLM mock slow, failing or never called.
- [ ] AC21 Free-text answers are normalised and de-duplicated, batched within the per-call budget (at most 10,500 characters per map call, at most 10 map calls); the reduce call receives no raw answers.
- [ ] AC22 When free-text entries exceed the cap, a deterministic sample is used and the result states "AI themes based on {N} of {M} free-text answers."; otherwise no such notice.
- [ ] AC23 The AI step is asynchronous: the create response has AI status `running` with progress; reads report progress; any failed call fails the whole AI step with its specific error while the statistics stay available and unchanged; an AI step `running` over 780 seconds is reported as `analysis_interrupted`.
- [ ] AC24 Charts render for single-choice, multi-choice and scale questions with a text table alternative, as soon as the analysis exists, including while the AI step runs or after it failed.
- [ ] AC25 LLM output is rendered as plain text only.
- [ ] AC26 Every LLM call (map and reduce) is labelled with the ADM-06 usage feature `ADM-03` (the value ADM-06 reserves for this feature).
- [ ] AC27 The response-count endpoint returns the exact number of responses of a form to admins only, with 404 for an unknown form, and does not change ADM-02 endpoints.
- [ ] AC28 When responses were submitted against different versions of a form, aggregates are grouped by question id using each response's own stored snapshot, with the deterministic rule in plan.md.
- [ ] AC29 The LLM mock never receives a choice or scale answer (option ids, option labels as answers, or scale values) in any call.
- [ ] AC30 Every analysis is stored with statistics, AI result or failure record, snapshot and label mapping; none is expired or pruned automatically (only an explicit delete by its owner removes one); the history list is paginated newest first with the page contract in plan.md.
- [ ] AC32 An admin can delete their own analysis (DELETE returns 204); the row with its statistics, AI result or failure record, snapshot and label mapping is removed; afterwards GET, evidence and the list give the uniform 404 or omit it.
- [ ] AC33 The delete endpoint rejects unauthenticated requests (401) and non-admin or disabled users (403) before any lookup or deletion.
- [ ] AC34 Deleting another admin's analysis, an unknown or malformed id, or an already-deleted id gives the same 404 `analysis_not_found` and changes nothing; the other admin's analysis is still readable by its owner.
- [ ] AC35 Deleting an analysis whose AI step is running is refused with 409 `analysis_running` giving seconds run and seconds until it is marked interrupted; nothing is deleted; once complete or failed it can be deleted.
- [ ] AC36 Deleting an analysis leaves the feedback responses, forms and ADM-06 usage counters unchanged.
- [ ] AC37 A delete racing with the background job's final write never resurrects the row and neither request raises an error: job writes are conditional on the row existing and running.
- [ ] AC38 The delete dialog (role alertdialog) names the form and creation time, the number of responses, states what is permanently deleted and what is not, has initial focus on "Cancel", traps focus, closes on Escape or Cancel with focus back on the trigger, and the delete request is sent once per confirmation.
- [ ] AC31 Every failure uses its own exact message from "Failure messages" with its step and numbers; the stored failure record keeps `code`, `message`, `step` and `details`; no message uses catch-all wording and none contains response content or identity.

## Out of scope
- Sending messages or follow-ups from an analysis (ADM-04), editing forms or responses, applying suggestions.
- Browsing or searching all responses (ADM-02-04), cross-form analysis, export, sharing, re-running or editing a saved analysis, cancelling a running AI step, automatic pruning or expiry of analyses, bulk delete, undo or restore of a deleted analysis or response, deleting forms, notifying a respondent that their response was deleted.
- Redacting names typed in free text; streaming; cost dashboards (ADM-06 counts calls).
- Changes to the shared LLM client or its limits; tool calling.
- Charts for free-text questions; a heavy chart library.

## Security and AI safeguards
- Access: all endpoints use the existing `admin_user` dependency (401 unauthenticated, 403 non-admin or disabled). Authorisation to review a response is the ADM-02-04 rule; ADM-03 reuses that function. Rows are filtered by `admin_id` in the query, including the delete statement (`DELETE ... WHERE id AND admin_id AND ai_status <> 'running'`), so one admin can never delete another's analysis. The background job re-reads only the stored snapshot ids and never accepts new ids.
- Evidence access is limited to labels stored in the analysis; the evidence endpoint never accepts a response id.
- Untrusted input: response text and model output are untrusted. Prompts wrap each entry in delimiters and state that content is data; the control is the server: strict JSON parsing, schema validation for map and reduce replies, evidence label subset checks (map: labels of that batch; reduce: labels appearing in the partial themes), length caps, plain-text rendering, no tools and no write path from a reply.
- No credentials, ids, respondent identity or choice and scale answers are sent to the LLM (AC14, AC29).
- Limits: 500 responses; entries cut to 280 characters; at most 10,500 characters per map call and 9,000 per reduce call; map `max_tokens` 1600 (per-attempt timeout 30 s), reduce 3000 (per-attempt timeout 60 s); at most 22 calls per analysis (each of 11 steps may be tried twice; about 13 analyses a day within the 300-call quota); one running AI step per admin; shared client limits 20 per minute and 300 per day per caller (`admin:{admin_id}`, so about 27 full analyses of AI a day; statistics are not limited by this); 15 s timeout and one provider retry per call; 780 s AI bound. A rate-limit or busy error fails the AI step with its wait time; the app never retries.
- Logs hold ids, counts, step, error class and reference ids only, never response text or model output. Failure messages and details hold only codes, step, numbers, schema field names and labels such as R7.
- Concurrent requests (same time): the same `request_id` twice, or two different submits from one admin, produce at most one running AI step (unique index on (admin_id, request_id) and a partial unique index on `ai_status = 'running'` per admin). Job progress and completion writes are conditional on the AI status still being `running`, so a late job cannot overwrite an analysis already marked interrupted. Reads are independent and read-only.
- Normalisation: ids in `response_ids`, `form_id`, `request_id` are trimmed, lowercased and parsed as UUIDs, then deduplicated keeping first occurrence; `expected_count` must be an integer from 0 to 500; `limit` and `offset` of the history list must be integers in range; an evidence label is trimmed and uppercased and must be `R` followed by 1 to 500 without a leading zero, else 404 `evidence_not_found`; free-text answers are trimmed, whitespace collapsed and compared case-insensitively for de-duplication. Malformed values give 422 `invalid_request` with its reason.

# Spec: ADM-02 Targeted feedback forms

Status: Approved by user in chat, 2026-11-07 (including respondent draft/resume amendment).

## Goal
Admins prepare targeted surveys, publish private inbox requests, close collection and review preserved responses.

## Local runtime prerequisite
ADM-02 API operations require the configured PostgreSQL database to be reachable and migrated through `20261107_adm02_feedback`. In local development, start the backend with `uv run --env-file .env uvicorn main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000 --reload` from `src/backend`, so the process uses the same `DATABASE_URL` as the migration command. A 503 message (`The feedback service is temporarily unavailable. Try again later.`) indicates an unavailable storage/configuration dependency; it does not mean the ADM-02 routes are intentionally disabled. The UI preserves its documented retry behavior while the operator checks the backend, database, migration and frontend proxy.

## Behaviour
An active admin manages forms under `/admin/feedback`. Drafts can be saved, reopened and edited. A form has a title, optional description, ordered questions and at most one optional feature or published-lesson association. Publish requires a saved, valid draft and a reviewed selection of individual active students or individual active teachers (one role per form). Publication freezes the form and recipient set and atomically creates one unread survey request per recipient. Later account creation or course changes never expand the audience. A lesson association is metadata only: it neither expands the recipient set nor grants access to lesson content. The admin selector accepts only currently published, nonwithdrawn lessons; respondents may receive a survey about a lesson without receiving permission to open that lesson.

Student and teacher survey inboxes are provided at `/student/inbox` and `/teacher/inbox`; this is the minimum survey-request delivery surface, not general messaging. Unfinished responses automatically save as private server-side drafts. When the same student or teacher returns, their saved answers are restored so they can continue. Required questions may be unanswered in a response draft. Only explicit successful submission completes the form for that recipient; it validates required answers, freezes the submitted response and prevents further editing or submission. This recipient completion state is separate from the admin's draft/open/closed collection state.

Closing a published form permanently ends draft editing and submission; previously saved drafts remain private and do not become collected responses. Admins review submitted responses while open or closed, never unfinished drafts. Raw answers never appear in inbox lists, activity context or AI requests. An intended respondent may retrieve only their own unfinished draft while collection is open; completed or closed form reads contain no answers. Admins see submitted respondent identity under the proposed identified-feedback policy below. No deletion or reopening action is provided.

## Acceptance criteria
The following criteria are copied from `docs/UserStories.md`, with IDs for test mapping.

- [ ] AC01 **Supported question types:** I can add short-answer and paragraph text questions; multiple-choice and dropdown questions that accept one answer; checkbox questions that accept multiple answers; and linear-scale questions where a respondent selects one value within the scale's configured range.
- [ ] AC02 **Choice options:** For multiple-choice, dropdown, and checkbox questions, I can enter the answer options respondents can choose from.
- [ ] AC03 **Linear-scale setup:** For a linear-scale question, I can configure its range and optionally label its endpoints.
- [ ] AC04 **Required questions:** I can mark each question required or optional. Respondents cannot submit a form while a required question is unanswered.
- [ ] AC05 **Form association:** I can optionally associate the form with a feature or lesson.
- [ ] AC06 **Persistence:** When I save and reopen a draft, its questions, required-field settings, and optional association are unchanged.
- [ ] AC07 **Publication restriction:** A draft is unpublished and cannot be opened for response submission, including by direct request.
- [ ] AC08 **Scope boundary:** Drafting does not send inbox requests or make the form available to respondents; publication and recipient selection are covered by ADM-02-02.
- [ ] AC09 **Audience review:** Before publication, I can review whether the form is being sent to the selected student or teacher audience.
- [ ] AC10 **Inbox delivery:** Publishing creates an in-app inbox request with a link to the form for each intended recipient.
- [ ] AC11 **Access restriction:** While the form is open, only intended recipients can view and submit it; other users are denied through both the interface and direct requests.
- [ ] AC12 **Submission eligibility:** A form can receive responses only after publication and before it is closed.
- [ ] AC13 **Scope boundary:** This story distributes the form request; it does not cover composing a separate follow-up message or announcement (ADM-04).
- [ ] AC14 **State change:** I can close a published form to end response collection.
- [ ] AC15 **Submission restriction:** After closure, the form rejects new responses through both the interface and direct requests, including requests from intended recipients.
- [ ] AC16 **Existing data:** Closing the form preserves responses already submitted; authorised admins can still review them.
- [ ] AC17 **Scope boundary:** Closing does not delete the form or its responses. Reopening a closed form is outside this story.
- [ ] AC18 **Response association:** Each submitted response is shown with its form and the questions it answers.
- [ ] AC19 **Question snapshot:** The wording and required status of each question at the time of submission remain available with the response; later form edits do not alter that record.
- [ ] AC20 **Access restriction:** Only an authorised admin can review collected responses.
- [ ] AC21 **Scope boundary:** This story provides direct review of form responses. It does not generate AI summaries or select evidence for an analysis (ADM-03).

Additional acceptance criteria requested by the user on 2026-11-07:

- [ ] AC22 **Resume unfinished forms:** Student and teacher answers automatically persist as a private response draft and restore on return, refresh, a later authenticated session or another device, while collection remains open. This includes incomplete required questions and all six question types.
- [ ] AC23 **Draft visibility:** An unfinished form is labelled `Draft` for its recipient after the first successful save; it is excluded from submitted response counts and admin response review. Other users, including admins, cannot read its answers.
- [ ] AC24 **Completion:** Only successful explicit submission changes the recipient status to `Completed`. Submission preserves the final answers atomically and prevents further draft writes, editing or duplicate responses, including concurrent requests.
- [ ] AC25 **Save failures:** Saving, saved and failed-save states are explicit. Failed saves retain the current answers in the open page and offer manual retry; unsaved changes are never presented as saved. Normal in-app navigation flushes pending changes before leaving. Closure or loss of access blocks further saving/submission.

- Requests per load and per retry: One request for each list, detail, response list, response detail or recipient form load; zero for a new draft. Opening an existing editor sends one form read and one association-options read; new editor sends one association-options read. Association options are fetched once, not per keystroke. Audience review sends one paged audience read, explicit search/role/page changes send one each; review confirmation makes no read. Each save/publish/close/submit sends one mutation; success uses its response to update the page, no automatic re-fetch. Recipient form GET also marks its request read atomically; no second mark-read request. Each `Retry` repeats only the failed request once. Development effect replay must not duplicate reads. Recipient draft autosave follows the plan: one PUT after 1000ms idle, coalesced serial writes, one flush before normal in-app navigation, zero writes on hydration, and manual retry after failure. Submission waits for an in-flight save, cancels pending debounce and posts current answers once. No polling or automatic retries.
- Wrong role: Admin routes redirect students/teachers to their own authenticated landing area using replace and send zero ADM-02 requests. Recipient routes redirect admins to their landing area and student/teacher cross-role paths to their own landing area, using replace and sending zero feature requests. Direct backend requests remain forbidden.
- Link history: push or replace: Sidebar links, list-title links, `New feedback form`, `Review responses`, response links and `Back to inbox` push. First successful create replaces new-form URL with saved detail URL. Save/publish/close/submit, dialogs, pagination and filtering do not change URL. Authentication redirects replace.
- Refresh: Saved data reloaded with the request counts above, including the current recipient's saved draft answers. Admin unsaved editor changes reset; keep its existing unsaved-changes warning. Respondent draft answers live on the server, never in browser storage. Show `Draft saved. You can return to finish later.` only after acknowledgement; use native beforeunload protection while a save is pending/failed. Abrupt browser termination or offline navigation cannot guarantee delivery of unacknowledged edits; saved drafts remain recoverable.
- Back and Forward: Re-entering a route reloads current authorised state once; never restore private response content from client cache. List pagination resets to first page. Previously closed/submitted forms show current terminal state.
- Expired session or logout: Abort pending feature requests and timers, clear loaded private data and unsent fields, and replace with sign-in using the existing authenticated-session flow. Acknowledged response drafts remain on the server and restore only for the same authorised recipient. Never block logout to finish a save. 401 message is `Your session has expired. Sign in again.` A late response must not repopulate data after account change.
- Backend down: Show `Cannot connect to the server. Check your connection and try again.` with `Retry` on reads; preserve unsent fields on mutations; reconcile uncertain admin writes or submissions through the plan reload controls before another write. Recipient draft-save failures offer Retry saving draft; stale-version protection prevents overwrite. Do not assert a timed-out mutation failed to commit.
- Longest wait before an error: 15 seconds per feature request, then abort and display `The request timed out. Check the current form before trying again.` Pending mutation controls are disabled; recipient navigation waits for draft flushing as specified in the plan. Following an uncertain admin save, publication, close or respondent submission, explicit reload discovers server state before another state-changing action; recipient draft PUT instead permits manual retry guarded by its version, with C06 requiring explicit reload; recipient submission uses Check submission status and the plan reload confirmation before discarding local edits.
- No generic failure message: Each failure uses the plan dictionary; network copy is reserved for transport failures. Unexpected HTTP 500 uses `The feedback service encountered an error. Try again later.` Unknown HTTP statuses use the same honest service-error copy, not network copy.

## Out of scope
AI analysis, feedback follow-up, announcements, email/push, anonymous responses, deletion/export/retention automation, reopening, editing published form definitions, multiple submissions, editing completed responses, attachments, branching questions, admin form-definition autosave and a full general inbox. STU-05/STU-06/TEA-06 are not claimed complete by this delivery; their required full stories remain separate.

## Security and AI safeguards
Every endpoint checks an active authenticated session and the required role. All active admins are authorised to manage/review submitted feedback. Only snapshotted recipients can read/save/submit published forms. Unfinished answers are visible only to their owner while collection is open; submitted raw answers and respondent identities are admin-only. Hidden/nonrecipient/unpublished-form IDs return the same 404. Respondents cannot impersonate another account by supplying an owner ID. Associated lesson reads expose title and course metadata only, never unpublished content or protected answers. Association validity is checked at save and publication; no associated lesson body is returned. Disabled/archived recipients lose access; retained response identity uses immutable user ID, with a stored display-name snapshot.

No SoC LLM calls occur. Feedback is plain untrusted text, rendered as text, never HTML or instructions. Do not add feedback to the study companion context. No credentials, answers or question text in security logs. Persistent server storage and additive migration required; no memory-only records. List results bounded, request/answer limits and quotas fixed in plan. Sensitive responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`; client data is cleared on logout. Respect keyboard controls, reduced motion, and 390px mobile layouts without hidden controls.

- Concurrent requests (same time): Optimistic version checks prevent lost admin-draft and recipient-draft updates independently. Recipient saves and submission serialise on the same form/recipient state; stale saves cannot overwrite newer answers or recreate drafts after completion. Submission validates the current draft version and atomically records final answers, removes the draft and completes the recipient. Publication is atomic with recipient/inbox creation; retries cannot duplicate requests. Draft saves and submission serialise with close, so they either commit before close or are rejected after close. Concurrent close is idempotent. Account eligibility changes serialise with publication/save/submission checks. Submitted snapshots and responses never mutate after commit.
- Normalisation: Form/question/option/response/inbox UUID identifiers parsed canonically; account IDs remain exact opaque strings of 1-64 characters; malformed IDs produce 404. Trim titles, prompts, options, labels and association values, reject blank values and duplicate options after casefold comparison. Preserve response-draft text exactly for restoration; trim final text-answer boundaries before required validation at submission. Do not alter inner whitespace or text case. Checkbox option IDs are unique and sorted by configured option order before storage. Unknown keys, foreign question/option IDs, wrong scalar types including boolean-as-integer, repeated question IDs and oversized bodies are rejected.
